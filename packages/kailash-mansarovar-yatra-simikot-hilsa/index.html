The air route to Kailash cuts out the long drive across the plateau. You fly from Kathmandu to Nepalgunj, then to Simikot in the far west of Nepal, and on to Hilsa on the Tibet border. From Purang the road leads to Lake Mansarovar and Darchen.

Small-plane and helicopter flights depend on the weather, so the itinerary includes spare time. The price is confirmed when you enquire.

Highlights

  • Flights over western Nepal to Simikot
  • Puja at Lake Mansarovar
  • Three-day parikrama of Mount Kailash

Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 01 Kathmandu to Nepalgunj

    Afternoon flight to Nepalgunj.

  2. Day 02 Fly to Simikot

    Morning flight to Simikot. Rest and short walks to adjust to the altitude.

  3. Day 03 Simikot to Hilsa, cross to Purang

    Helicopter to Hilsa, cross the border and drive to Purang.

  4. Day 04 Rest day at Purang

    A day to get used to the altitude.

  5. Day 05 Lake Mansarovar

    Drive to Lake Mansarovar for puja and the parikrama of the lake.

  6. Day 06 Kailash parikrama: Yam Dwar to Dirapuk

    Walk about 12 km to Dirapuk.

  7. Day 07 Kailash parikrama: over the Dolma La

    About 22 km over the pass to Zuthulphuk.

  8. Day 08 Kailash parikrama ends, drive to Purang

    Walk to the end of the parikrama and drive to Purang.

  9. Day 09 Back to Simikot

    Cross to Hilsa and fly by helicopter to Simikot.

  10. Day 10 Simikot to Kathmandu

    Fly to Nepalgunj and on to Kathmandu.

  11. Day 11 Departure

    Transfer to the airport. This day also covers any flight delays.

What is included

Included

  • Hotel stays on twin sharing, as listed in the itinerary
  • Breakfast and dinner (vegetarian)
  • Private vehicle with driver for all road travel
  • Help from our team throughout the tour
  • Domestic flights and helicopter in Nepal
  • Chinese group visa and Tibet permits
  • Tibetan guide and support vehicles in Tibet

Not included

  • Lunch, drinks and personal expenses
  • Puja, special darshan and donations at temples
  • Travel insurance
  • Anything not listed under inclusions
  • Ponies and porters for the Kailash parikrama
  • Extra nights if flights are delayed
