Do Indian citizens need a visa to visit Nepal?

No. Indian citizens do not need a visa for Nepal. Carry a valid passport or a Voter ID card issued by the Election Commission of India. Children should carry an identity document as well.

Do Nepali citizens need a visa for the Chardham Yatra?

No visa is needed. Carry your citizenship certificate or passport. Every Char Dham pilgrim must also be registered with the Uttarakhand government, which we do for you.

When are the Chardham temples open?

Usually from late April or May until October or November. The opening dates are announced each year on Hindu festival days, and we plan departures around them.

What is the best time to visit Muktinath?

March to June and September to November. The temple is at 3,710 m, so winter brings snow and very cold mornings, and flights to Jomsom are often delayed in the monsoon.

Can Indian passport holders do the Kailash Mansarovar Yatra through Nepal?

The rules for Indian passport holders are set by the Chinese authorities and can change from one season to the next. Send us an enquiry and we will tell you the current requirements before you book.

How do I pay for a booking?

Indian travellers pay in Indian rupees by UPI or bank transfer. Nepali travellers pay in Nepali rupees by eSewa, Khalti or bank transfer. An advance confirms the booking and the rest is paid before travel.
