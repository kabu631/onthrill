Pumdikot Shiva Temple

A large statue of Lord Shiva on a hilltop above Phewa Lake, with views over Pokhara and the Annapurna range.

Pokhara, Gandaki Province, Nepal

About Pumdikot Shiva Temple

Pumdikot is a hilltop in Pumdi Bhumdi, on the southern side of Phewa Lake in Pokhara. A large seated statue of Lord Shiva holding his trident stands above a temple hall at the top, and it can be seen from much of the city.

The terrace around the statue looks over the lake, Pokhara and, on clear days, the full Annapurna range. Sunrise and sunset are the best times to go. The road climbs most of the way and is followed by a short walk.

How to reach Pumdikot Shiva Temple

About 45 minutes by car from Lakeside, Pokhara, then a short walk.
